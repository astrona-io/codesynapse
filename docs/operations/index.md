# Administration

!!! info "In plain words"
    "Running" CodeSynapse means keeping a handful of programs alive on a computer or a server: the website you click around in, the service behind it, the databases that hold the data, and the helpers that do the slow work. These pages explain how to start them, who may do what, how to update safely, and how to connect your coding assistant. If a word is new to you, look it up in the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md).

    **Starting from nothing?** Read in this order: [Requirements](requirements.md) (will it fit on my machine?), [Running the stack](running.md) (start it), [First run and sign-in](first-run.md) (log in and set up your team), then [Claude Code bridge](claude-code-bridge.md) (connect your assistant).
